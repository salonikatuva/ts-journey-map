Backstage · System & data
Workday critical-role flag. 439 critical roles, 73% CG80+, and 41% of critical-role incumbents are "too early to rate" on performance.
TTV templates carry the profile work, but shared success profiles are largely absent, so "what good looks like" isn't written down.
Workday · Talent Cards · Talent Finder. Talent-card completion 18.1% of 66,190; internal-fill varies 93.8% to 47.6% across markets; "talent isn't treated as a Philips-wide asset."
Readiness stages Ready Now / ACR / ANR. Of covered roles: 31% Strong · 45% Concerns · 24% Weak. 62% of all CG80+ have no Ready Now successor.
Development programmes exist but funding decreased and several are paused. Nominations carry no attached development actions, so identification never compounds into readiness.
Workday plan vs actual hire. Conversion 12% in 17 months; CG90+ external-hire churn ~20% (vs <5% company-wide). The study notes conversion "cannot be calculated with the current process."
Recency: only 11.3% of CG80+ plans updated in 6 months (89% stale). No outcome tracking. Benchmarks (Microsoft, Amazon, Unilever, J&J, PepsiCo) run board-owned, yearly-cadence, readiness-based governed pipelines.
